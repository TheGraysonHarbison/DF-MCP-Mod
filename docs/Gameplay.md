
# Advice For Food/Drink:

The average dwarf requires two meals and two drinks a month to be happy:

Minimum Food = 2 * 12 Monthes * Total Population * 1.5

Minimum Drink = 2 * 12 Monthes * Total Population

Drinks are brewed in bulk on average unlike food:

1 Food Order = 1 Food.

1 Brew Order = 10 Drink.

# Keeping Extra Storage Handy:

As your fortresses production grows, you will often run out of storaage fast.

Keeping orders of spare barrels and bins will combat this. You should always have at least
15 empty barrels handy and 10 empty bins ready as well.


# Advice For Trade:

Two things determine an items value. A) How processed it is and B) its ingredients.

Prossessing refers to how many steps it took to reach an end product. A Clear-Glass Table takes several 
steps more than a Wooden Table thus provides more value.

Cut Gemstones are the best item to sell. They are stored in bins which are easy to move, have immense value
for what they are and are light enough to be able to sell lots of them. You should always strive to have a box of cut Gemstones to sell by the autum

Never sell Dwarven made artifacts. Doing so greatly angers the creator of said artifact and removes a 
large chunk of your fortresses collective wealth.

You should avoid buying heavily processed things with the rare exception being instuments.

Purchasing animals from a caravan is the easiest way to start farming them as opposed to catching them, but
Dinosaurs are very rare and better caught.

# General:

- Wealth should be generated by the fort and never imported.
- High Quality Furniture and Engravings are the easiest ways to boost a rooms weath metric.
- If you employ Cage Traps, you need a stock of empty cages to load them with.
- Miners should never be kept idle.
- Never give in to the demands of tree-huggers.
- Offices should be put in rooms.
- Dwarves are happiest if underground.
- Being rained on sours a dwarfs mood.
